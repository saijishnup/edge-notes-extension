# Edge Notes Extension

A simple and elegant browser extension for taking quick notes, built for Microsoft Edge.

## Features

- **Quick Notes**: Easily jot down notes and ideas.
- **Dark Mode**: Switch between light and dark themes for comfortable viewing.
- **Persistent Storage**: Your notes are saved locally and are available whenever you open the extension.
- **Edit and Delete**: Full control to edit or delete your notes.
- **Copy to Clipboard**: Quickly copy note content.

## How to Use

1.  **Add a Note**: Type your note in the text area and click "Add".
2.  **View Notes**: Click on "Recent Notes" to see your saved notes.
3.  **Edit a Note**: Click on any note in the list to open the editor. Make your changes and click "Save".
4.  **Delete a Note**: Click the "X" button next to a note to delete it.
5.  **Toggle Theme**: Click the 🌗 icon to switch between light and dark mode.
